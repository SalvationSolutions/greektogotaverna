Our Story

A Hidden Gem in Quezon City

Tucked along a quiet street in Cubao, Greek To-Go Taverna was born from a love story — a Filipino-Greek couple sharing their passion for everything Greek with the people of Manila.

Every dish comes from old family recipes, made with fresh ingredients imported directly from Greece. From Shrimp Saganaki to Keftedes meatballs, Spanakopita and Baklava — each bite is a journey to the Mediterranean.

Available for dine-in or order via GrabFood and WhatsApp, the taverna also carries authentic imported products: extra virgin olive oil, Greek salad mix, and hand-crafted olive oil soaps.
